About

Found Time began the way most good things do, without a plan. Two longtime friends, both buried in other lives, kept circling back to their piano bench whenever a free hour turned up.

What started as a cover, traded back and forth for the fun of it, became a reason to keep finding the hour. For now, that means love ballads built on piano and voice, with the particular blend that only happens when two people have known each other long enough to stop performing. Original songs are next; the covers are just where the hour started.

"We're not chasing a sound. We're chasing the hour we almost didn't have."
