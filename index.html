About

Found Time began the way most good things do, without a plan. Two longtime friends, both buried in other lives, kept circling back to their piano bench whenever a small pocket of time turned up.

What started as a cover, traded back and forth for the fun of it, became a reason to keep finding the time. For now, that means love ballad covers built on piano and voice.

“We're not chasing a sound. We're chasing the time for ourselves to make what we love.”
